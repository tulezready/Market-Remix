Sell your products beyond your district.

If you run a business in East New Britain, you can apply to sell on MaketPles ENB. The Division of Commerce & Industry checks every application, so buyers know each business listed is a real, verified local enterprise.

01

Fill this in

About ten minutes. You need your business details, a phone number, and photos of what you sell.

02

We check it

If anything is missing we phone you. We do not reject applications over small gaps.

03

You start selling

Once approved you get your own login to manage prices, stock and orders.
