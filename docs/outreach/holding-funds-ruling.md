# Request for advice — collecting and paying out SME sales

**Send to:** first the Division's finance and legal officers (and the Provincial
Treasurer if a government account is used). If they cannot answer item 3, send the
same questions to the **Bank of Papua New Guinea — Payments System Oversight**
(https://www.bankpng.gov.pg/regulation-and-supervision/payments-system-oversight).

**Why this matters:** the National Payment System Act 2013 requires a Bank of PNG
licence to act as a *payment service provider*. A licence requires at least
K500,000 capital and keeping customers' money separate from the operator's own.
A marketplace that collects buyers' money and pays it on to sellers later may fall
within this. We need a clear answer **before** taking real payments. *(This note is
a summary for discussion, not legal advice.)*

---

**Subject:** MaketPles ENB — how sale proceeds are collected and paid to SMEs

Dear [Name],

MaketPles ENB will let verified East New Britain SMEs sell online. We propose the
following money flow and ask for advice on whether it is permitted, and how it
should be structured.

**Proposed flow**
1. A buyer pays once for a basket (possibly from several SMEs) through a licensed
   payment gateway ([gateway]).
2. The money settles into **one account** held by [entity from D1].
3. The platform records, per order, what each SME is owed: the sale value less a
   platform fee of [x]% and any agent commission of [y]%.
4. After delivery and a [n]-day holding period, the SMEs are paid by bank transfer
   every [frequency]. Payout runs are approved by two named officers.
5. Agents who take cash orders pay the cash into the same account.

**Questions**
1. Can this account be a government (or trust) account, and which Financial
   Management Act or provincial rules apply to holding money that belongs to SMEs?
2. How should the platform fee be treated — revenue to the Province, or to the
   operator? Does it attract GST?
3. Does this arrangement require a payment service provider licence under the
   National Payment System Act 2013? If so, could it instead be provided by the
   licensed gateway or bank (for example, if they hold the funds and make the
   payouts on our instruction)?
4. What records and reconciliation will the Auditor-General expect?

We would appreciate a written reply by [date].

Kind regards,

[Name], [Position]
