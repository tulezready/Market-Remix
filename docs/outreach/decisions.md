### D1. Merchant of record and ownership

The merchant of record is the legal entity that holds the payment gateway contract
and the bank account buyers' money is paid into. It is named on the receipts, the
Terms of Use and the domain registration.

**Agreed in planning (8 October 2026): the Division** — Division of Commerce & Industry,
East New Britain Provincial Administration — is the merchant of record. It owns the
merchant account, the settlement bank account and the domain (registrant), and pays the
running costs directly. The developer is the technical contact only. Confirm by signing below.
