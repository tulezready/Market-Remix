**Subject:** Merchant enquiry — online payments for MaketPles ENB, East New Britain's official SME marketplace

Dear [Merchant Services / Business Banking team],

The Division of Commerce & Industry, East New Britain Provincial Administration, is preparing to launch **MaketPles ENB**, the official online
marketplace of the East New Britain Provincial Administration's Division of Commerce
& Industry. It lets verified small and medium businesses in the province sell
products online to buyers in East New Britain, elsewhere in Papua New Guinea and
overseas. We plan to launch in November 2026, with a launch cohort of about 35
businesses.

We would like to accept online payments through your gateway and ask for the
following:

**Payment methods**
1. Which methods can buyers use: Visa/Mastercard, local ATM/debit cards, BSP Pay,
   mobile money (e.g. CellMoni), USSD?
2. Can overseas buyers pay with foreign cards? In which currency are they charged,
   and who carries the exchange cost?

**Costs and settlement**
3. Set-up fee, monthly fee and per-transaction fee (percentage and/or fixed).
4. How long until funds reach our account, and into which banks?
5. Refund and chargeback process, fees and timeframes.

**How it works technically**
6. Do you provide a **hosted payment page** (so card details are never entered on our
   site)?
7. Do you send a **server-to-server confirmation** (webhook or callback) when a
   payment succeeds or fails, and can we query a payment's status?
8. Is there a **test (sandbox) environment** and integration documentation?

**Marketplace questions**
9. Our model: the buyer pays once for a basket that may include several businesses;
   we pay each business its share on a schedule. Can your service **split payments**
   between businesses, or support **bulk payouts** to many bank accounts?
10. Are there any licensing or account-structure requirements for a marketplace that
    pays out to other businesses?

**Onboarding**
11. What documents do you need (IPA certificate, IRC TIN, bank account, Terms of Use
    and refund policy on the website)?
12. How long does approval usually take?

We would be grateful for a reply by [date — one week from sending], as our launch
timeline depends on this decision.

Kind regards,

[Position], [Entity]

On behalf of the Division of Commerce & Industry, East New Britain Provincial Administration
