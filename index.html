Far from home? Send it home.

Working in Moresby, Lae, or overseas? Buy on your card from anywhere, name a recipient here in East New Britain, and they collect from the SME or receive local delivery. Built for the wantok system — supporting family shouldn’t need a money transfer office.

1

Fill your basket

Browse and add products as normal.

2

Choose “Send home” at checkout

Enter your recipient’s name and PNG mobile number.

3

Pay from anywhere

Card checkout works from any country. No PNG account needed on your side.

4

They’re notified by SMS

Your recipient collects from the SME or receives delivery.

Ol agen · Inclusion

No card? No problem.

No smartphone either? A trusted local agent completes the order on the buyer’s behalf — and earns a commission for doing it.

01

Order through someone you trust

Agents take orders in person at trade stores, market stalls and community houses, then complete checkout for the buyer.

02

Cash in, goods out

Buyers pay the agent in cash or by EFTPOS; the agent settles the payment digitally through the platform.

03

A livelihood, not just a service

Every completed order pays the agent a commission — designed for people already trusted in their own communities.

The Division

Who stands behind this

The Division of Commerce & Industry is the arm of the East New Britain Provincial Administration responsible for growing the province’s business sector — from business inspections and licensing through to SME development and economic data.

MaketPles ENB extends that mandate online, with the Division standing behind seller verification and fair trading — supported by the national agencies and financial institutions working alongside the province’s SME programme.
