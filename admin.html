How a payout works. Money is held while an order is being prepared and for the holding period after it is collected, then it is ready to pay. One officer prepares a payout — the database picks the orders and adds them up. A different officer approves it; whoever verified the business's bank details cannot. After the bank transfer is made, record the bank reference to mark it paid. Payment is only possible to a verified bank account.
