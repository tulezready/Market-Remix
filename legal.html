Part 1

Terms of use

In short: the businesses on MaketPles sell to you directly; we check who they are, take your payment on their behalf, and help if something goes wrong.

1. Who we are

MaketPles ENB (“MaketPles”, “we”) is an online marketplace for businesses in East New Britain Province, run by [merchant of record] for the Division of Commerce & Industry, East New Britain Provincial Administration. By using the site or placing an order you agree to these terms.

2. How the marketplace works

  • Every seller is an independent business in East New Britain. Your purchase is made from that business; it is responsible for the goods, their description and their quality.
  • The Division checks each seller before they can list, and reviews every listing before it appears.
  • We collect payment on the seller’s behalf and pay it to them after your order is fulfilled.
  • A basket with items from several businesses becomes one order per business, linked by one basket reference.

3. Prices and orders

  • Prices are in Papua New Guinea Kina (PGK) and are what you pay for the item. Delivery is shown separately at checkout.
  • The minimum order is K15. Delivery costs K15, and is free on orders over K150.
  • Prices and totals are always calculated by our system when the order is placed. If a price shown in your browser is out of date, the order uses the current price and you see it before paying.
  • An order is accepted once the seller confirms it. A seller may decline an order — for example if an item has sold out or a fresh product is not ready — and any money you have paid is refunded in full.

4. Paying

  • Cash through an agent: pay a MaketPles agent using your order number. The agent gives you a receipt.
  • Bank transfer: pay into the MaketPles account shown at checkout, using your order number as the reference.
  • Card or BSP Pay (when available): you pay on the bank’s or payment provider’s own secure page. We never see or store your card details.
  • Orders not paid within 3 days may be cancelled.

5. Collection, delivery and Send home

  • You can collect from the business, collect from an agent, or choose delivery in the areas offered at checkout.
  • With Send home, you pay and someone in East New Britain receives the order. Give their correct name and PNG mobile number; we send them the order details by SMS. They may be asked to show the order number when collecting.
  • Fresh produce must be collected within 2 days of being ready, other goods within 14 days. After that the order may be cancelled; fresh goods that have spoiled cannot be refunded.

6. Using the site fairly

Please don’t place orders you don’t intend to pay for, use someone else’s payment details, give false recipient details, or try to interfere with the site. We may cancel orders or block use of the site where this happens.

7. What can’t be sold

Sellers may not list anything illegal to sell in Papua New Guinea, including protected wildlife or wildlife products, firearms and ammunition, drugs and unlicensed medicines, alcohol without the right licence, counterfeit goods, or anything that breaks someone else’s rights. The Division may remove any listing.

8. Our responsibility

We work hard to keep the site running and the information accurate, but we cannot promise it will always be available or error-free. We are not the maker of the goods sold. Nothing in these terms takes away rights you have under the laws of Papua New Guinea, including consumer protection law.

9. Changes, law and contact

We may update these terms; the version and date at the top of this page show when. Orders are governed by the terms in force when they were placed. These terms are governed by the laws of Papua New Guinea. Questions: contact the Division.

Refunds & returns

In short: if it’s wrong, damaged or never arrives, you get your money back. Tell us quickly, with a photo if you can.

What happenedWhat you getTell us within
You cancel before payingNothing to refund — the order is simply cancelledAny time
The seller cancels or can’t supplyFull refund, including delivery—
Never arrived, or not ready when promisedFull refund, or a new date if you prefer7 days
Damaged, wrong item, or not as describedReplacement or full refund48 hours (fresh produce: 24 hours)
You changed your mindUp to the seller; usually not possible for fresh, food or made-to-order itemsBefore collection or delivery

How to ask for a refund

  1. Contact the Division (or your agent) with your order number.
  2. Say what is wrong and, if you can, send a photo.
  3. We check with the seller and tell you the outcome within 5 working days.

Sometimes we will ask for an item to be returned to the seller or an agent before refunding. If the problem is the seller’s, they pay the cost of returning it.

How you are paid back

  • Card or BSP Pay: back to the same card or account.
  • Bank transfer: back to the account you paid from.
  • Cash through an agent: through the agent, or by bank transfer if you prefer.

Refunds are made within 10 working days of being approved. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

If you’re not happy with the outcome

Ask for the Division to review it. You can also contact the Independent Consumer and Competition Commission (ICCC). Your rights under Papua New Guinea law are not affected by this policy.

Part 3

Privacy

In short: we collect what we need to get your order to you and pay the seller. We don’t sell your information, and we don’t track you around the internet.

What we collect

  • When you order: your name, mobile number and (if you give it) email; the recipient’s name and mobile number for Send home; a delivery address if you choose delivery; what you ordered and how you paid.
  • When a business applies or sells: contact person, phone, email, IPA registration and TIN, bank account details, and photos of the business and its products.
  • On your device: your basket is kept in your own browser so it is still there when you come back. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use analytics.

We never receive card numbers. Card payments happen on the payment provider’s own page.

Why we use it

  • To take, prepare and deliver your order, and send you SMS updates about it.
  • To pay sellers and agents, handle refunds, and keep financial records.
  • To check businesses before they sell, and to prevent fraud.
  • To report on the programme — only as totals, never naming buyers.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we don’t use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • The seller of your order: your name, mobile number, and the collection or delivery details — only for that order.
  • The agent who placed or hands over your order.
  • Division staff who run the marketplace.
  • Service providers we use to run the site: Supabase (database, hosted in Sydney, Australia), Cloudflare (website hosting), Google Fonts (typefaces), and our SMS and payment providers. They may handle your information only to provide their service.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

A business’s phone, tax and bank details are never shown publicly. Only the business itself and the Division can see them.

Where it is kept, and for how long

Your information is stored in Sydney, Australia, the closest secure data centre to Papua New Guinea. Order and payment records are kept for 7 years for financial-record purposes. Business applications that are not approved are deleted after 12 months.

Keeping it safe

Access is limited by role: a seller can only see their own business and orders, and only named Division staff can see private business details. Connections to the site are encrypted.

Your choices

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted where we don’t need to keep it for an order, a refund or the law. Contact the Division, and we will reply within 30 days.

Part 4

Seller terms

In short: list honestly, prepare orders on time, and we pay you what you’re owed — minus a fee you can see on every sale — into your business bank account.

1. Who can sell

Businesses operating in East New Britain Province, approved by the Division. You need IPA registration and a bank account in the business’s name. The Division can help with registration if you aren’t formalised yet.

2. Your listings

  • Describe products honestly, with your own photos, and keep prices and stock up to date.
  • The Division reviews each listing before it goes live and may ask for changes or remove it.
  • Prices you set are the price the buyer pays for the item.
  • Don’t list anything from the “What can’t be sold” list in the Terms of use.

3. Orders

  • Confirm or decline each order within 24 hours, and have it ready by the date you give.
  • Only hand over goods once the order shows as paid.
  • Use a buyer’s details only to fulfil their order. Don’t contact them for marketing or share their details.

4. Fees

FeeAmountWhen
Joining and listingFree—
Platform fee10% of the item totalEvery paid order
Agent commission5% of the item totalOnly on orders placed through an agent

Delivery charges paid by buyers are not part of your sale. Every order shows the item total, each fee, and what you receive.

5. Getting paid

  • Your money becomes available 5 days after an order is collected or delivered — time for any problem to be raised.
  • Payouts are made fortnightly to your business bank account, with a statement listing every order included.
  • Your bank details are confirmed by phone before the first payout. If you change them, payouts pause until the new account is confirmed — this protects you from fraud.
  • Each payout is prepared by one Division officer and approved by another.

6. Refunds and problems

If a buyer is refunded because of a problem with your goods or a missed order, the amount comes off your next payout. If a refund is not your fault, you are not charged. You can ask the Division to review any decision.

7. Tax and your business

You remain an independent business and are responsible for your own tax (IRC), licences, food-safety and other obligations. Statements from MaketPles can help with your records.

8. Pausing or leaving

You can pause your stall or leave at any time; open orders must still be completed and you will be paid what you are owed. The Division may suspend a seller for false listings, repeated unfulfilled orders, misuse of buyer details, or selling prohibited goods, and will tell you why.
